##21# — Deactivates specific unconditional forwarding set by *21* .
##002# — A universal deactivation code that cancels all types of call forwarding on your line (unconditional, busy, no answer, and unreachable).
 *#21# - To see if call forwarding is enabled on your device.

The **code that works for call forwarding is determined primarily by your mobile network carrier** (e.g., AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile), not the model of your phone (e.g., iPhone, Samsung, Pixel).
Here is how to figure out which code will work for you:
1. Identify Your Mobile Carrier
Your carrier is the most important factor. Different networks use different standards:
Most International and GSM Carriers (AT&T, T-Mobile, Vodafone, etc.): Typically use the standard GSM MMI codes like **21*...# and ##002#.
CDMA Carriers (Verizon, US Cellular): Often use different activation codes, such as 72 for activation and 73 for deactivation.
2. Check Your Carrier’s Official Website
The most reliable way to find the correct codes is to search your carrier's official support documentation. They will list the exact sequences required for their specific network.
You can often find this information by searching their help centers, like:
AT&T Support: Call Forwarding
Verizon Support: Call Forwarding FAQs
T-Mobile Support: Call Forwarding
3. Use Your Phone's Settings Menu
Both iPhones and Android phones have built-in menus that automatically use the correct underlying code for your carrier when you toggle the feature on. This is the simplest method if you are unsure of the codes:
**On iPhone: Go to Settings > Phone > Call Forwarding.
On Android: The path varies by phone, but usually you go to Settings > Calling Accounts or Supplementary Services > Call Forwarding.**
Using the settings menu is an easy way to ensure compatibility without memorizing specific codes.
4. Trial and Error (Use with Caution)
You can attempt to use both the *21* and the *72 codes.
If the code works, you will usually receive a confirmation message from your carrier.
If the code is incorrect or unsupported by your network, you will hear a message like "The number you dialed cannot be completed as dialed" or receive an error message on your screen.
Remember to use the deactivation code (e.g., ##002# or *73) if you successfully activate a feature you didn't intend to keep.
